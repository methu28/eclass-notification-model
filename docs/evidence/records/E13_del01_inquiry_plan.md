## Verified original proposal

Page 4 defines urgency ordering as putting all urgent items ahead of all nonurgent items, with newest-first ordering within each group. This is urgency-class-first ordering, not sorting all deadlines nearest-first. The same page defines newest-first recency and a limited reserved-position hybrid. Page 10 identifies two reserved positions as the initial alternative, with one and three as additional comparisons. Proposed volumes of 10 and 40 and urgency-only ordering were not implemented in the completed model.

These definitions establish the proposed design, not York's algorithm. The dossier uses E13 to explain narrowing and clarify the methods. The grading feedback supplied for this revision identifies DEL01-4 as partially demonstrated because the three proposed ordering methods were insufficiently explained. The dossier now specifies these rules and distinguishes proposed from implemented conditions. No other feedback or change to the earlier grade is inferred.
